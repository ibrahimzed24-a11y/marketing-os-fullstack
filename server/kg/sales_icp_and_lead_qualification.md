# VALIOZ — Sales Knowledge Base

## 1. ICP & Ideal Customer Profile
- **Profile:** Owners/Principals of mid-market dental and beauty practices (15–60 team members), in Metro Vancouver, Metro Toronto / London, and Calgary-Edmonton.
- **Buying signal:** already spending on marketing tech OR actively renovating the patient funnel; owns the website decision.
- **ICP score bands:** 90+ = high-intent, 80–89 = warm, 70–79 = nurture.
- **Deciding factors:** 100% clinic-owned property (no SaaS rent), verifiable review evidence, 24/7 intake, CDCP readiness, clear ROI (new patients booked).
- **Rejection signals:** budget locked in one-year SaaS contract, practice owner wants "we'll decide later", no Google Business Profile presence.

## 2. Lead Qualification
- **Qualification = ICP score >= 75** → treat as a real lead, prioritize for outreach.
- **Status flow:** `Ready - needs approval` → `Sent` → `In progress` → `Qualified` → `Delivered`.
- **Lead data is ground truth.** Never fabricate review counts, rankings, or follow-up dates. If a field is missing, mark it `unknown` and flag for harness update.

## 3. Sale Motion
- **Opening:** 2-minute diagnostic call: "What brings you in today?" → 5 listening questions → present the one-pager (offer) that answers the top diagnosis signal.
- **Objection handling (top 3):**
  - "We're happy with the current site/website." → "Got it — let's look at the last 90 days: calls, bookings, and where they're dropping off."
  - "It's too expensive." → anchor to cost of lost patients vs the one-time setup fee.
  - "We need to think about it." → book a review cadence, send the one-pager, quote back in 10 working days.
- **Close:** one-click proposal + a 5-day review window; every outbound must reference a verified lead source.

## 4. Counselling & Tone (Sales)
- High-trust, consultative, no hype. Name the diagnosis then the fix. Use spoken scripts for WhatsApp/voice with spoken numbers written out in words and NO markdown/bullets/emojis.
- Every outbound must trace to a verified lead record (source: research, harness file, or doctor referral) — no cold-list spraying.
