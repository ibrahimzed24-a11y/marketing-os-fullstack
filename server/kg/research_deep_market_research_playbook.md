# VALIOZ — Deep Market Research Playbook

## 13. When to launch `deep_market_research`
- Before any competitive teardown, ROI claim, or "we have demand" statement on a call.
- When scoping a new market (Metro Vancouver, Metro Toronto/London, Calgary-Edmonton) or a new offer line.
- Before writing SEO/GEO deliverables: ground keyword intent, SERP features, and local 3-pack density.

## 14. Go-to-market (the 5-tool engine)
1. **google_search_console** — query impressions, average CTR, average SERP position, Page 2 fast-leap (positions 8–15) opportunities.
2. **keyword_planner** — exact search demand volume, YoY velocity, commercial competition tier, High/Low Top-of-Page CPC.
3. **tag_manager** — container tag audit (GA4 core, Google Ads enhanced conversions, Meta CAPI), conversion tracking integrity, funnel leak.
4. **seo_engine** — competitor DR, backlink footprint, allintitle KGR (allintitle / search volume < 0.25) fast wins.
5. **geo_matrix** — local 3-pack competition density, regional metro intent indices (0–100), suburban vs downtown dynamics.

## 15. Research Report Contract
- Every report ends with: **Verdict:** Ready to Deliver.
- Grounded in harness & tools: yes (5 assigned tools).
- Sections: executive summary → market sizing → SERP + search-intent mapping → competitor/teardown → whitespace → prioritized actions.
- Never fabricate: live tool values are the only quoted numbers; screenshots or tool responses are the source of record.
