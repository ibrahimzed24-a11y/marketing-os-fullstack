# VALIOZ — Offer Framework & Pricing

## 9. Offer Architecture (the "one offer, three envelopes")
- **Setup Fee:** $499 one-time — website + AI intake engine build (deliverable: custom mobile-first clinic website with online intake).
- **Guarantee:** 30-day risk-free launch; $0 upfront until 10 new patients booked.
- **Retainer:** $299/month optional AI Growth Agent after day 30 (content, review, optimization); clinic owns everything.
- **Asset ownership:** clinic owns domain, hosting, code, connected Google profiles. Nothing rented.

## 10. Deliverables per Envelope
| Envelope | Deliverables |
|---|---|
| Website build | Custom responsive site + online intake form |
| Local presence | GSC + GBP 3-pack optimization, GBP posts, review velocity |
| AI intake | 24/7 answering, CDCP qualification flow, daily growth brief |
| Creative | WhatsApp card, ad creatives, email sequence (600–680px) |
| Audit | SEO/GEO forensic teardown + prioritized gap list |

## 11. ROI Framing (sales)
- Frame price as "new patients" not "service". One new new-patient appointment ≈ the setup fee + 2 months of retainer.
- Use verified review/rank evidence in every case; re-verify live before quoting.

## 12. Pricing Edge
- Many competitors rent clinics a website. VALIOZ hands over ownership on day one. That is the deciding argument for 50%+ of clinic owners.
