# VALIOZ — Sales Playbook

## 5. Outreach Cadence (per lead)
- **Day 0:** Diagnostic call (15 min) + send one-pager.
- **Day 3:** Value follow-up (one insight, one question).
- **Day 7:** Proposal + review window (5 working days).
- **Day 14:** Break-up/nurture email; re-engage on new buyer signal.
- **Weekly:** review book of all qualified leads; dedicate 2 hours to the top ICP.

## 6. Channels
- **WhatsApp/voice:** spoken word, max 20 words/sentence, numbers as words (see `voice_ai`-skill).
- **Email:** single-column, 600–680px, one CTA, unsubscribe footer (see `open-design email`-skill).
- **LinkedIn:** comment-first engagement; no hard pitch; one personalised DM after 3 genuine comments on their content.
- **Search:** verify we have the REAL search-intent demand for the offer (use `deep_market_research` skill) before claiming demand.

## 7. Verification Rules (sales integrity)
- Review metrics must be screenshot-verified or pulled from a live tool. Never quote "4.9★, 1,600 reviews" from a meeting — re-verify live and record the source URL.
- Every claim in a proposal cites a source (harness file, live tool, or KB article).
- If a tool ping returns 403/404, mark the field `unverified` in the CRM — do not guess.

## 8. Next Move Rule (make the next move obvious)
- **Every deal has exactly one owner and one next move.** "Next move: send proposal by EOD; review call T+5."
- A deal with no next move is not a deal — it is a task for the coordinator.
